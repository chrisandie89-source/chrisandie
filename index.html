Why the Prism

The Prism

The prism is a study in potential. It receives light and reveals what was already contained within it.

Every idea arrives whole but undivided — a single beam. The work is to hold it at the right angle: to separate what's tangled, to see the parts that were always there, and to reveal possibilities that weren't immediately obvious.
